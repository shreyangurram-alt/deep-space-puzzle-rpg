# Voidbound: The Starfall Ledger

A browser-based RPG prototype where player progression is driven by puzzle-solving instead of combat.

## Features

- Deep-space sci-fi setting with narrative exploration
- RPG stat system: Logic, Engineering, Empathy, Resolve, Vitality
- Leveling and XP progression
- Branching story decisions and puzzle-based challenge checks
- No traditional combat; survival depends on solving environmental and social puzzles

## How to play

1. Open `index.html` in a browser.
2. Choose the best action for each chapter based on your character stats.
3. Solve the station's puzzles to keep the ship intact and reach the ending.

## Objective

Advance through the derelict station, survive its hazards, and complete the Astral Ledger challenge by using your character skills to solve the mysteries of the void.
